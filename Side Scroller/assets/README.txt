CLEAN INDIVIDUAL SPRITE PACK

These files were individually quality-checked from the transparent source atlas.
Every exported sprite:
- has a transparent background
- is centered on a 256x256 transparent canvas
- has padding around the artwork
- was selected to avoid the visibly cut/half-frame problem from the prior ZIP

Folders:
shiavon/             Normal-form character frames
bendera/             Powered-form character frames
enemies/             Stage 1 street-thug frames
effects_powerups/    Effects, power-ups, flag and related assets

NOTE:
The generated source atlas itself contains a few frames where neighboring artwork
or labels are fused into the same transparency component. Those visibly damaged
frames were deliberately NOT included rather than shipping broken PNGs.
